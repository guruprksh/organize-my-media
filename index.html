Apply the result to your folder?

Get a move script for your real files

Your browser cannot move files itself. Save this as sort.sh, run it inside the folder you picked, and it creates a Sorted folder. Nothing is deleted; duplicates go to a review folder.

With Claude vision on, a small preview of each photo (and one frame per video) is sent to Claude in batches to pick a folder. Claude first looks at a sample and designs folders that fit your library, then files every item; anything unclear goes to Other. With it off, or where Claude vision isn’t available, files are only grouped by type, screenshots and month. Duplicates are exact matches only. Up to 500 files are drawn; all are counted and sorted.
